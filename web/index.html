L — Line (SketchUp pencil): click a vertex, edge or face to place a point; the second click draws a dashed guide chord (it is also cut into the mesh, invoked with L or the FreeCAD Sketcher chord G, L; hold Shift to constrain strictly to the nearest axis (90°, Draft-style); but never exported to STL). A chord lying on a flat face becomes a boundary for Extrude. Hold Shift to lock direction; type a length + Enter for an exact point. E — Extrude the selected face: drag it with LMB or type mm; Join adds, Cut removes material in either direction. P — Point. Esc exits any tool. Hover an edge — it highlights and a point runs along it; click to open the popup (offset / radius, scroll wheel over the fields edits live), click the dot to place it and drag. Drag an edge body to move the whole edge (Sketcher-style). Drawn lines are hoverable too: the point runs along them and magnetizes to their midpoint — start a new line there to split a region in half. Yellow — vertices: drag them or type exact X/Y/Z. Cyan — edge midpoint. Alt while dragging an edge point bends the whole edge as an arc (radius in the tooltip); without Alt it folds. Ring of identical vertices (all teeth at once): Alt+grab a vertex (Blender loop-select) or double-click it, then drag; the ring moves in its own plane (radius), press Z to move height instead. Shift+grab makes it a column — same radius on both caps at once (e.g. move pocket apexes without tilting the walls). Double-click a face selects the whole flat region bounded by model edges and drawn lines. X/Y/Z keys during a drag lock one axis; points snap to other vertices (green) and edges (red). Everything works in all four views; ortho drags stay in the view plane, wheel zooms. Ctrl+Z undoes (30 steps). After manual edits the STL button exports the edited mesh from the browser. All units are millimetres: 0.1 mm edit step, 1 & 10 mm grids.
