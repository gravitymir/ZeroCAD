L — Line (SketchUp pencil): click a vertex, edge or face to place a point; the second click draws a dashed guide chord (it is also cut into the mesh, but never exported to STL). A chord lying on a flat face becomes a boundary for Push/Pull. Hold Shift to lock direction; type a length + Enter for an exact point. P — Push/Pull: hover a flat face, drag with LMB to extrude along its normal (0.1 mm steps); walls follow. P or Esc exits any tool. Hover an edge — it highlights and a point runs along it; click to open the popup (offset / radius, scroll wheel over the fields edits live), click the dot to place it and drag. Yellow — vertices: drag them or type exact X/Y/Z. Cyan — edge midpoint. Alt while dragging an edge point bends the whole edge as an arc (radius in the tooltip); without Alt it folds. Ring of identical vertices (all teeth at once): Alt+grab a vertex (Blender loop-select) or double-click it, then drag; the ring moves in its own plane (radius), press Z to move height instead. Shift+grab makes it a column — same radius on both caps at once (e.g. move pocket apexes without tilting the walls). Double-click a face selects the whole flat region bounded by model edges and drawn lines. X/Y/Z keys during a drag lock one axis; points snap to other vertices (green) and edges (red). Everything works in all four views; ortho drags stay in the view plane, wheel zooms. Ctrl+Z undoes (30 steps). After manual edits the STL button exports the edited mesh from the browser. All units are millimetres: 0.1 mm edit step, 1 & 10 mm grids.
